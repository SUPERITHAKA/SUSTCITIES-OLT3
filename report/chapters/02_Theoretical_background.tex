\chapter{THEORETICAL BACKGROUND}
\section{Urban stormwater}
Stormwater includes rainfall and snowmelt that runs over roofs, streets, and other urban surfaces. High runoff volumes and limited drainage capacity can contribute to flooding and water-quality problems \parencite{epaGreenInfrastructure}.

\section{Sustainable stormwater management}
Green infrastructure can manage runoff close to its source through processes including infiltration, retention, and evapotranspiration. Common approaches include rain gardens, permeable pavement, bioswales, and green roofs \parencite{epaGreenTypes}.

\section{Flood resilience and education}
Flood resilience concerns preparedness, risk awareness, and the capacity to respond and recover. The OLT introduces these themes through basic explanations and knowledge checks; additional educational scenarios may be developed following user testing.

\section{Accessibility of online learning}
The W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 provide testable success criteria for accessible web content \parencite{wcag22}. The project will use relevant WCAG 2.2 Level AA requirements as a target for evaluation. Using semantic HTML does not by itself establish compliance.
